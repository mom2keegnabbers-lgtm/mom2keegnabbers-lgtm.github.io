Privacy Policy — Breathing Helper

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Breathing Helper is a guided breathing exercise app by Marigold Hart, made for people living with COPD and asthma. It works fully offline and does not require an account to use.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal information through this app. There is no account, no sign-in, and no tracking.

The app keeps nothing about you. Your session settings (such as session length and pace) exist only while the app is open and are never saved or sent anywhere. The app stores no health information, no usage history, and no identifiers on your device or anywhere else.

Third parties

This app does not send your information to third parties, advertisers, or analytics services. The app requests no device permissions and contains no ads.

The one time the app interacts with another app is when you tap the "Call 911" button in calm-down mode: it opens your phone's dialer with 911 filled in, so you can place the call yourself. No information is sent to us when you do this.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.

Contact
