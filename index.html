Privacy Policy — Homemade Treats for Happy Dogs

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Homemade Treats for Happy Dogs is a cookbook app by Marigold Hart. It displays recipes and reference information. It does not require an account to use.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal information through this app. There is no account, no sign-in, and no tracking of what recipes you view.

The favorites (heart) feature in the app works only during your visit and is not saved or sent anywhere.

Third parties

This app does not send your information to third parties, advertisers, or analytics services.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.

Contact

For questions about this app, contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
