Privacy Policy — My Pet's First Year

Last updated: October 30, 2026

My Pet's First Year is a pet care tracker app by Marigold Hart. It lets you keep pet profiles, daily care logs, health and vaccination records, contacts, and notes for your pets. It works fully offline and does not require an account to use.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal information through this app. There is no account, no sign-in, and no tracking.

Everything you enter in the app — such as your pets' names, care check-offs, weight and vet-visit logs, vaccination records, contacts, and notes — is stored only on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, that information is deleted with it.

Third parties

This app does not send your information to third parties, advertisers, or analytics services. The app requests no device permissions and contains no ads.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.

Contact

For questions about this app or this policy, contact the developer at mom2keegnabbers@gmail.com, or through the Play Store listing.
